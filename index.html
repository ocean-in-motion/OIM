Questions

About the photography.

How many images will I get?

It depends on the session length and type, but most half-day sessions deliver 40–70 finished images. We'll agree a rough number before the shoot.

Do you photograph nervous or reactive pets?

Yes — sessions are built around your animal's comfort, with plenty of breaks, treats, and no forced poses.

Do you shoot on location or in a studio?

Both. Portraits and headshots can be studio or on-location; weddings and pet sessions are almost always on location.

Questions

About the videography.

How long is a typical wedding film?

A highlight film usually runs three to six minutes; a full ceremony edit is delivered separately for those who want it.

Do you offer photo and film together?

Yes — most weddings and larger sessions are booked as a combined package, with one visit covering both.

When will I receive the final film?

Typically four to eight weeks after the session, depending on the length and time of year.
