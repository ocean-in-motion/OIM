Case study — Automotive Entertainment Brand

K.C. & Roscoe

An automotive entertainment brand built around a raccoon mascot named Roscoe, from character design to animation to the link hub that ties it together.

Who it's for

K.C. & Roscoe is an automotive entertainment brand with a mascot at its center. They needed a recognizable character, content that brings him to life, and one place to send every follower.

What we did

  • Branding
  • Character design for Roscoe the raccoon
  • Animation
  • Video production
  • Social media setup and marketing
  • Custom link hub website

The O.I.M. approach

One mascot, one look and one link: every video and post points back to the same brand. That's the idea behind Ocean In Motion: branding, website and marketing built together as one system, so each piece makes the others stronger.
