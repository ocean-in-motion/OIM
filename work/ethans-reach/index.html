Case study — Nonprofit Organization

Ethan's Reach

Brand, website and launch support for a Georgia nonprofit focused on driver's education and community safety.

Who it's for

Ethan's Reach is a Georgia 501(c)(3) nonprofit offering driver's ed courses and community safety events. They needed a brand and a website that could carry the mission and make it easy to get involved.

What we did

  • Brand development
  • Custom website
  • Social media setup
  • Educational video production
  • Setup and approval of the nonprofit's online accounts

The O.I.M. approach

The site was built in 2026 as a fast, single-page experience that puts the courses, events and ways to get involved front and center. That's the idea behind Ocean In Motion: branding, website and marketing built together as one system, so each piece makes the others stronger.
